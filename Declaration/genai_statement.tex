The author discloses the following uses of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI), distinguishing assistance with thesis preparation from the research uses reported in DriveLM.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Tools for thesis preparation.} Large-language-model assistants, including an assistant integrated in the Cursor code editor, were used for literature research, writing, LaTeX preparation and automated feedback on drafts.
  \item \textbf{Thesis-preparation tasks.} These included converting the LaTeX sources of the author's publications into Chapters~\ref{ch:occnet}--\ref{ch:kai0} and their appendices; restructuring text, labels and citations; drafting and editing the Abstract and Chapters~\ref{ch:intro}, \ref{ch:background}, \ref{ch:methodology}, \ref{ch:discussion} and~\ref{ch:conclusion}; checking bibliographic metadata; producing LaTeX and TikZ code for figures and tables; and providing critical feedback on the draft. Automated feedback is not an official thesis examination.
  \item \textbf{Research uses in DriveLM.} GPT-3.5 was used to translate and refine question--answer text, with manual checks described in Appendix~\ref{drivelm:app:nus_collection}. GPT-based scores were also used to evaluate model answers, as described in Section~\ref{drivelm:sec:metrics} and Appendix~\ref{drivelm:app:metrics_vqa}. These scores are model-generated judgments, not independently observed physical driving or robot performance.
  \item \textbf{Research and responsibility.} The experiments reported in this thesis are those described in the cited research publications. The thesis-preparation assistants did not carry out those experiments or produce replacement experimental measurements. The author remains responsible for the originality, accuracy, attribution and interpretation of the thesis. Final verification of the revised text, numerical claims, citations and contribution statements against primary sources is required before submission; this statement does not certify that this verification has been completed.
  \item \textbf{Approval.} The author reports that supervisory approval has been obtained. The approval date and scope will be inserted from the approval record before submission.
\end{itemize}
