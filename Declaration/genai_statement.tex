In accordance with the policy of The University of Hong Kong on the use of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) in research postgraduate theses, the author discloses the following.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Tools.} An AI assistant based on large language models, integrated in the Cursor code editor, together with its literature-research, writing and reviewing extensions.
  \item \textbf{Tasks.} (i) Converting the LaTeX sources of the author's publications into the core chapters (Chapters~\ref{ch:occnet}--\ref{ch:kai0}) and their appendices, including restructuring, management of labels and citations, and figure layout; (ii) drafting and editing text for the Abstract and for Chapters~\ref{ch:intro}, \ref{ch:background}, \ref{ch:methodology}, \ref{ch:discussion} and~\ref{ch:conclusion}; (iii) searching for and verifying bibliographic metadata for the literature review; (iv) producing LaTeX and TikZ code for figures and tables; and (v) an internal critical review of the draft.
  \item \textbf{Not delegated.} The research questions, methods, experiments, data and results reported in this thesis originate from the author's research and publications. No experimental result in this thesis was produced by GenAI.
  \item \textbf{Verification and responsibility.} The author has reviewed all text, numbers and citations against the original publications and sources, and takes full responsibility for the content of this thesis.
  \item \textbf{Approval.} The use of GenAI for the tasks above was approved by the supervisors on \authorcheck{date of approval}.
\end{itemize}
